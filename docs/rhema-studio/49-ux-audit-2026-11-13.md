## 3. Target information architecture

The rule: **Live is for running the service. Connections is for everything you plug in. Design is
for how things look. Settings is for preferences of this computer.** This is doc 45 §2A; the app
has drifted from it.

### 3.1 Navigation model

```
WORKSPACE            YOUR CHURCH
  Home       ⌘1        Connections        (devices, OBS, cameras, screens, other computers)
  Prepare    ⌘2        People & access    (today's Permissions + Team)
  Live       ⌘3      ─────────────
  Library    ⌘4        Settings   ⌘,      (new sidebar row, above the desk identity)
  Design     ⌘5
  History    ⌘6        [avatar] name · Main computer   (identity, appearance, help only)
```

- One level of navigation in the sidebar. One level of tabs inside a page. Anything deeper is a
  list with a detail pane, or its own page.
- Every instruction of the form "go to X → Y" becomes a button that goes there. A small registry
  (`src/studio/places.ts`: id, label, open()) feeds those buttons and Find anything, so labels
  cannot drift (S5, S6).
- Find anything indexes every place in the registry, including OBS, Cameras, Scenes, Timer.

### 3.2 One home per task

| Task | Home | Also shown (same component, not a copy) | Leaves |
|---|---|---|---|
| Connect OBS (address, password, test) | **Connections → OBS** | Inline in the Live OBS tool when OBS is not connected | Settings → Remote Control (keeps a one-line link) |

| Add or pair a camera, camera router | **Connections → Cameras** | — | Live → Production tools → Cameras (setup part) |

Height budget: 44 status + 12 + 36 panel header + 258 picture + 28 caption + 12 + 56 transport +

**Hierarchy**

| Level | Controls | Treatment |
|---|---|---|
| Primary | Take | The only filled teal button on Live. 44 px high, names item and destination. Enter. |
| Primary status | LIVE pill, Program edge | Red word + dot + panel edge, only when at least one output shows it. Otherwise "Not on any screen" in amber, with the outputs popover one click away. |
| Secondary | Previous, Next, Verse, Restore, tool tabs, Rehearsal switch | `outline`, 44 px. |
| Danger | Clear; Black | Clear: red words on an outline button (as now), right-aligned, separated from Take by space. Black: outline with a filled square icon; always visible. Neither is ever adjacent to Take. |
| Tertiary | Status-bar pills (Screens, REC, OBS, Crew, bell) | Pills that open popovers. No raw numbers until opened. |
| Hidden until needed | OBS and Cameras tabs | Absent until OBS is turned on or a camera exists. Their off-state lives in Connections. |

**Behaviour changes implied** (each needs its own check against §7's invariants):

- The Take result goes to a fixed-width slot at the right of the transport row (or a toast), not
  into the toolbar. Failures stay until dismissed and also go to the tray.
- The recorder becomes a pill ("● REC 13:07", "Recorder off", "Recorder: check") with a popover
  holding Start/Stop, the destination, and a "Details" disclosure for frames, bitrate, peak and file
  size. "No picture for 5 seconds" is the attention line.
- Auto-live moves into the Listening tool, with its three options and the explanatory text that
  Settings → Heard verses already has. While it is On, a persistent amber "Auto-live on" pill shows
  in the status bar.
- Esc closes a popover or leaves a text field. It does not collapse or close the tools dock (the
  dock no longer closes), so the Esc-Esc Clear gesture has no neighbours.
- The crew strip moves to the foot of the running-order column and collapses to one line when
  nobody is online.

## 5. Design-system rules

Use what exists. The work is mostly deleting variants.

### 5.1 Tokens that exist (`src/index.css`, Tailwind v4 `@theme inline`, `:23-112`)

Colours, radius (`--radius: 0.5rem` and its derived scale), fonts (IBM Plex Sans, Source Serif 4,
Geist Mono), `--control-height`, the `touch-hit` utility, the `compact` variant, tone tokens
(`tone-neutral|accent|green|amber|rose|blue`), kind tokens, `on-air`, `live-pulse`,
`confidence-high|mid|low`. There is **no type scale and no spacing scale**; sizes are policed only
by `src/text-size-guard.test.ts`. Geist and Inter are imported (`:4`, `:8`) and unused.

### 5.2 Type scale (add as `@theme` tokens; five steps, no arbitrary sizes)

| Role | Size | Tailwind | Replaces |

### 5.5 Status pills (`src/studio/ui/status-chip.tsx` is the one component)

Today: `StatusChip` (5 tones), `Badge` (12 variants), a second local `Badge` in

### 5.7 Panels and tabs

- **One tab level per surface.** If a tab's content needs tabs, it is a page or a list-detail.
- Two styles only, both from `src/components/ui/tabs.tsx`: `line` for a page's sections, `default`
  (segmented) for a view switch of up to four options inside a panel. Retire the five hand-rolled
  tablists (`production-workspace.tsx:127`, `design-workspace.tsx:94`, `library-workspace.tsx:439`,
  `scene-design-picker.tsx:236`, `text-properties.tsx:487`).
- A tab change never changes its container's height.
- Every panel uses `PanelHeader` (`src/components/ui/panel-header.tsx`, today used by five legacy
  panels only): title on the left, at most two actions on the right, the rest in an overflow menu.
  No control sits against the title without a gap (L5).
- Dialogs do not open dialogs. The output editor becomes a second step inside the Outputs dialog.
- Explanatory text: at most one sentence under a title. Longer explanations go behind a "How this
  works" disclosure or into Help.

### 5.8 Technical telemetry

Three tiers, always in this order:

1. **State**: one pill. "REC 13:07", "OBS connected", "Main Display on".
2. **Exception**: one plain sentence, only when something is wrong, with what to do. "No picture
   from Main Display for 5 seconds. Check the screen is on."
3. **Details**: behind "Details" in the pill's popover: resolution, frames written and dropped,
   bitrate, audio device and peak, file size, disk free. The same numbers appear in full on
   Settings → Diagnostics.

Nothing from tier 3 appears on a surface a volunteer sees by default.

## 7. Risks

- Slice 3 touches the file that attaches the Live key handler (`present-workspace.tsx:132-188`).
  Keep the handler and its guards untouched and move only the JSX around it.
- Removing the embedded `ProductionWorkspace` changes what `openProductionTab()` means for its
  callers (OBS chip, Connections, Home, Health). Map every caller to a new place before deleting.
- Hiding the OBS and Cameras tabs until configured must not hide a *lost* connection: once turned
  on, the tab and its pill stay and show `error`.
- Moving Auto-live must keep its state visible. A hidden control that changes the screens is worse
  than an ugly one.
- The recorder pill must still surface "no picture" and disk-full within the same time as today.
