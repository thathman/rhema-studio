- **Branch / PR:** `cam-052/smooth-viewfinder` (from `main` 174208b). Acceptance 10fm.
- **Behaviour:** `Gpu`: the look runs once per camera frame: frames only the encoder takes are graded straight into the encoder surface; frames the viewfinder or histogram take are graded into an FBO at the camera/encode size, then blitted to the encoder first and to the viewfinder at screen size (a full-size blit every frame measured slower on the Adreno 505, so the hybrid). Viewfinder: SurfaceView (hardware overlay, no HWUI/GPU composite), `eglSwapInterval(0)`, skipped while its last frame's EGL fence (API 29+, `Fences`, flushed and polled with `eglClientWaitSync`, let go after 200 ms because this driver never signalled plain `eglGetSyncAttrib`) is pending or the last frame's grade+encoder ran over 90 % of the frame, drawn at least 4×/s. UI: level redraws only on a visible change (≤ 15 Hz), the status line and controls change views only when what they show changes. Camera input buffer = encode size; viewfinder buffer = the SurfaceView (screen) size.
