Comms & timing

Private team messages, acknowledgements, service clocks, and Stage cues have separate jobs from the congregation's live output.

Core service paths in repositoryFull operator and device acceptance in development

Private Comms

The current service code includes private notices and acknowledgements, with a held-to-talk voice path and role/channel routing. Comms is for authorized production-team coordination. Its messages and voice are not audience content.

  • Private voice must stay out of screens, PA, OBS program audio, and service recordings.
  • Use the intended role and channel; host permissions control who can send or receive.
  • Do not treat a sent message as proof that the intended operator saw or acknowledged it.

Acceptance status: browser microphone permissions, reconnect behavior, simultaneous speakers, and real headset/tablet operation have not been accepted as a complete production workflow.

Clocks, timers, and cues

The host owns the service clock and event state. Timekeeper controls are scoped by role. A Stage cue is a private reminder for its assigned recipient; it should never switch an audience scene or alter OBS program output.

History now includes a read-only plan-versus-actual event timing review from the saved run checkpoint and journal. An active event is shown only through its last checkpoint; incomplete or recovery-truncated timing remains unknown. The broader approved timing direction also includes expected timing, event transitions, host-configured reminders, and private Stage messages with urgency and expiry rules. The complete Timekeeper experience and physical Stage receiver behavior remain in development. See History & run review for details.
