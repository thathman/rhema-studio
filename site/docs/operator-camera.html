OPERATOR GUIDE

Camera operator

Keep assigned camera feeds healthy and report readiness without taking over scenes or OBS.

Camera role and router source in repositoryPhone and real-camera QA pending

Camera role responsibilities

The Camera role is scoped to camera work. The host camera router receives named feeds; the camera role may publish its assigned view and work with camera boxes in scenes where authorized. A healthy stream, a volunteer marked Ready, and a camera being on-air are different states.

Production boundaries

  • A Camera operator does not Take a congregation scene or switch OBS.
  • Camera routing to OBS and congregation outputs is separate; each output has its own scene and authorization.
  • Hiding a camera from scenes can affect more than one view, so broad changes need a host-side impact review.
  • Device health and network loss should be reported as observed; do not infer that an image is reaching every receiver.

Rhema Studio Cam

The repository includes an Android Cam application path for encrypted camera publishing to a local Rhema host. Phone compatibility, sustained video quality, heat, battery, reconnect, and actual camera-to-output behavior still require device-specific acceptance.

No public camera screenshot here: existing camera setup captures include LAN addresses, QR codes, and pairing-token parameters. They are intentionally excluded. A safe current device screenshot is not available, so the connected camera page remains marked in development.
