ROLE GUIDES

Operator pages

Each role has a defined responsibility and a bounded set of host-authorized actions.

Important: these guides describe current role limits and approved direction. The roles are checked by the host API, but the complete, responsive browser operator app and real-device acceptance for every role are still in development.

Choose a role guide

Bible operatorFind, preview, activate, and step Scripture within the Bible role. Worship operatorPrepare and show songs and hymns with the worship role. TimekeeperFollow the host clock and manage authorized timing actions. Screens & graphicsManage scoped congregation outputs and service-only graphics drafts. Stage operatorCoordinate private stage messages and host-assigned recipients. Camera operatorManage authorized camera sources and scene camera boxes without taking an output or switching OBS. OBS & streamingKeep OBS's online program separate from congregation production. Editor operatorContribute to service preparation without taking outputs live. Viewer operatorRead service and status information without production control. Presenter & ConfigureOperate the main desk and understand the host's greater authority.

Shared rules

  • The host assigns roles and remains the authority for service state, output scope, pairing, pause, and revoke.
  • A role can only perform actions accepted by the host service for that role and its current scope.
  • Private Preview, approved changes, output Takes, and confirmation that a receiver displayed a frame are distinct states.
  • Comms notices and talkback are private team features; they are not congregation, Stage, or OBS content.

For secure local-network behavior, see Connected Operators / LAN Browser Access. For the status of unfinished role pages, see the Roadmap.
