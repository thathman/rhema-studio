OPERATOR GUIDE

Screens & graphics

Work within assigned congregation outputs, and keep drafts, Preview, approval, and Live Take separate.

Owner-approved editing policyPersistence and UI acceptance pending

Stay inside the assigned scope

A paired Screens role is limited by host-assigned congregation outputs. A Take should name its target. Group Takes require a distinct host grant, the authorized target set, and a clear impact summary; a Screens role alone does not provide unrestricted control of every screen.

Drafts are not reusable designs

  • Temporary, service-scoped scene and graphic drafts are allowed by default within the desk's assigned scope.
  • Drafts should survive restart and reconnect when the same service is reopened.
  • Permanent reusable Design or Scene saves require a host-delegated capability or a specific approved request.
  • Save, approval, Apply, private Preview, and Take remain distinct. Approval must not silently change Live.

Safe output workflow

  1. Confirm the service and the intended output.
  2. Make a service draft and inspect it in private Preview.
  3. Check who will see the result and whether an approval is required.
  4. Use an explicit authorized Take only after review.
  5. Verify the named receiver separately and recover only within the same output scope.

In development: the host-enforced distinction between temporary drafts and durable reusable saves, same-service crash recovery, and the complete Screens & Graphics page are not established just because the policy is approved.

See the Permissions and authority contract and the Screens visual review candidate. Visual proposals are not device acceptance.
