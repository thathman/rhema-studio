OPERATOR GUIDE

Timekeeper

Follow the host's service clock and use only the timing authority assigned to this desk.

Event clock role in repositoryFull reminder workflow in development

One clock authority

Rhema Studio's timing direction uses one host-owned clock and service schedule. A Timekeeper desk should read and control only the event or timer actions allowed by the host. Its role does not silently switch a camera, scene, congregation output, or OBS program.

Current role scope

The repository's Timekeeper role includes event-clock actions and scoped timer commands. The broader approved workflow includes planned, expected, and actual timing; event transitions; host-managed manual and automatic reminders; and private Stage messages configured by urgency. The complete role page, reminder controls, recovery behavior, and device acceptance are still in progress.

Private reminders

Timing cues intended for Stage or a backstage display must stay within the host-assigned recipient scope. They must not appear on congregation output, OBS, public audio, or a recording unless explicitly designed and authorized for that audience.

Owner-approved direction, not finished feature status: see the Timekeeper and timing-view contract for clock, reminder, and private-display requirements.

See Stage operator for private display responsibilities and Comms & timing for notices and timers.
