<Callout title="Rhema Studio" type="info">
This page describes Rhema Studio, the church fork of Rhema. **Built** means in the app on `main`;
**Planned** means not built yet; **Owner decision** means it waits for the church's owner. The
matrix below is taken from the host's own permission check (`Role::allows` in
`src-tauri/crates/api/src/keys.rs`), so a desk cannot do more than it shows, whatever its screen
offers. Rows checked on a real desktop run name their row of the
[desktop acceptance guide](https://github.com/thathman/rhema-studio/blob/main/docs/rhema-studio/08-desktop-acceptance-guide.md);
the rest are built, not yet checked on the desktop.
</Callout>

One computer, the **main desk** (the host), runs the service. It keeps every control whether or
not anyone joins: with no volunteers nothing is greyed out. Other computers (Production →
Operators → **Join a host**) and paired phones help, each with a **role** the host gives it in
Production → Operators or Settings → Remote Control. A role is a permission, not a job title.

## The roles

| Role (as Rhema names it) | For |
| --- | --- |
| **Timekeeper** | The event clock and the service timer; never a screen |
| **Bible** | A typed reference, its verses and the Bible source only |
| **Screens** | Congregation scenes only |
| **Present** | All of the above, and moving through the service |
| **Present and settings** | Present, plus the look, opacity, confidence and the on-air switch |

A phone paired with the QR code starts as **Present**. A device paired before roles existed keeps
full access (**Present and settings**).

permissions of the desk that presses it, so a Timekeeper can start an item but not show a verse.

## Safety rules for every desk

- **Take back control** stops a desk's commands at once without unpairing it; it still sees the
  service, notices and Talk. Commands from a paused desk are refused (checked, 10dq.3).
- **One service at a time:** a desk looking at another service than the host's is refused until it
  catches up (checked, 10dq.5).
- A Bible desk's verse reaches only the Bible feeds while a song, hymn, slide or quick screen is on
  the main screens.
- Losing a desk never changes the host. A desk that hears nothing for 5 seconds says
  **Last update N s ago** and dims its clock; it reconnects by itself.
- Every command from a desk goes into the service log with its name; notices and Talk never do.

## Checked on the desktop so far

On one Mac, with the joined desks simulated by their keys (no second computer): a Bible key cannot Hold (10ef.4),
a paused desk's commands are refused (10dq.3), a desk on another service is refused (10dq.5),
running late and the timer from a joined desk (10de.2, 10de.3), Timekeeper commands and a screen
command refused (10cu.2, 10cu.3), notices (10cu.8), priority talk and revoking a talking desk
(10dd.1, 10dd.2). Hold and Resume from a desk are partly checked (10ef.2, 10ef.3). A run with two
real computers is not yet checked (10cu.1, 10eq.3).
