A **scene** arranges one congregation screen: the verse in a box over the preacher's camera,
the lyrics on a looping video, a countdown before the service. Taking a scene changes how a
screen is laid out, never what is live: the next verse still goes wherever it went before, and
OBS and the other screens do not change.

## Where scenes are made

- **Production → Scenes** is the full composer (this tab was called *Programs* before).
  **New from** offers ready-made scenes: Full screen, Bible and lyrics, Lyrics on a picture,
  Lyrics on video, Camera with the Bible, Full camera, Camera in a corner, Sermon split and
  Welcome. **Save as preset** keeps your own arrangement.
- **Present → Scenes** (under Live, Standard view) takes saved scenes and has **New scene** /
  **Edit scene** for a basic scene without leaving the live console: a background, one content
  box, the lower third and a camera.
- **Prepare → an item → Look and scene** records the scene an item is meant to have. Present
  then has that scene picked (**For Preview**); you still press **Take scene**, unless the item's
  **Signals → When you Take it → Take its scene** is ticked.

## What a scene can hold

| Layer | What it shows | Status |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Content (feed) | Bible, songs, hymns, slides or pictures, or whatever is on air, in a look from Design | Built |
| Hold | Keeps one box on the verse it shows while the rest follows; **Follow live again** releases it | Built |
| Colour, picture | A colour or a Library picture | Built |
| Video | A Library video on a loop, without sound, under the words | Built |
| Camera | A church camera chosen by name (or a typed WHEP address under Advanced); fallback words while it is not playing | Built; fallback words Checked (10dc.4); a camera scene on a Google TV Checked (10dh.8) |
| Clock | The service timer, the time of day, a countdown to a time, to the next item or to a milestone | Built |
| Ticker, lower third | The running ticker or the name band, inside the box (the scene then owns it on that screen) | Built; a smooth ticker layer on a Google TV Checked (10eb.1) |
| Logo | The Brand Kit logo | Built |
| Language per box | Both languages, only the first or only the second, whatever the screen shows elsewhere | Built |
| Fade per box | Cut, or a 0.3, 0.5 or 1 second fade when a box changes to something new | Built |

## Taking a scene

- **Take** puts the scene on the chosen congregation screen, with a cut or a 0.3, 0.5 or 1 second
  cross-fade. **Back to its own feed** (**Own feed** in Present) returns the screen to full frame;
  **Restore** takes the last scene again.
- **Black** and **Logo** cover every screen and feed, scenes included.
- In **rehearsal** a Take shows in Rhema only (marked *In rehearsal*); every screen has its own
  scene again when rehearsal ends.
- Every Take goes into the service log with who took it.
- A joined desk with the **Screens** or **Present** role can take and release scenes
  ([main desk and volunteer desks](/docs/features/desk-control)).
