# Rhema Studio context

Rhema Studio organizes a church service as a planned run of show and activates its content through independent live sources and audience compositions.

## Service content

**Audio cue**:
A service item that plays a library audio asset through this computer's selected sound output. It has no congregation or OBS screen content.
_Avoid_: Audio image, sound slide

**Visual media item**:
A service item that presents a library image or video to the configured screen output. Its media is distinct from local audio cues.
_Avoid_: Picture/sound item
